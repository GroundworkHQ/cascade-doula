Body Ready Method®

Helping you move through pregnancy, birth, and postpartum with strength and ease.

What it is

What Is Body Ready Method®?

BRM® is an evidence-based approach that optimizes movement, alignment, and body balance to support a more functional pregnancy, efficient birth, and smoother recovery.

The method uses five pillars to train the body for pregnancy, birth, and recovery.

01

Upper Body Mobility

Read more

How freely the ribs, shoulders and upper back move affects breathing and how much room there is above the belly. Work here is about restoring range that pregnancy tends to take away.

02

Core

Read more

The abdominal wall and the deep muscles around it change shape through pregnancy. The focus is on how the core coordinates and supports, rather than on how strong it looks.

03

Pelvis

Read more

The pelvis is the passage the baby travels through, and how it sits and moves shapes that journey. Work here looks at alignment and at how freely the joints move.

04

Pelvic Floor

Read more

The muscles at the base of the pelvis hold weight through pregnancy and stretch during birth. The aim is a floor that both engages and releases, not one that is simply tight.

05

Movement Patterns

Read more

How you sit, stand, lift and rest all day adds up. Small changes to everyday movement can make more difference than any single exercise.

Is this for you?

Who Should Work with Me

  • You are pregnant and preparing for birth
  • You have experienced difficult births previously
  • You worry about core strength, diastasis recti, or pelvic floor concerns
  • You prioritize function over fitness

Why Work with a BRM® Pro?

Body Ready Method® Professionals understand how to support the pregnant body to find resilience, strength, and mobility. We are experts in pelvic mechanics and understand what to do during every stage of the birthing process.
